# Search and Ranking

## Search goals

Find the correct product/entity quickly and support natural-language shopping questions.

## Query types

- exact product
- partial product
- brand
- category
- feature
- natural-language need
- comparison question

## Ranking signals

Use a combination of:
- textual relevance
- exact identifier match
- brand match
- category match
- product popularity
- evidence volume
- freshness
- user filters

Do not let popularity override exact product identity.

## Filters

Category, brand, price, score, decision state, features, evidence volume, deals.

## Search suggestions

Group suggestions:
Products, Brands, Categories, Questions.

## Natural language

Queries such as '50 bin TL altında kamera iyi telefon' should become structured criteria before ranking.

## Comparison search

Recognize 'X mi Y mi?' and route to comparison where possible.

## Quality

Track zero-result rate, reformulation rate, click-through and product-detail conversion.

## Abuse

Rate-limit automated search and reject malicious query payloads.
